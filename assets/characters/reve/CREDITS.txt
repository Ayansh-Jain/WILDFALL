Princess Reve (reve.glb) is generated by the scripts in Art/reve/pipeline
(Blender 5.2 + MPFB) from a reference photograph supplied by the project owner.
Third-party sources:

- Body, eyes, eyebrows (eyebrow009), eyelashes (eyelashes02), teeth, ballet flats
  (toigo_ballet_flats) and the 52 ARKit face-unit shape keys: MakeHuman / MPFB
  community assets, CC0 (system assets released as CC0 in September 2020).
- Skin base texture: MakeHuman "young caucasian female" diffuse, CC0
  (recoloured to a warm light-medium tone; eyeliner, lid shadow, lipstick, blush and
  brow fill are painted by 02_skin.py; pores and roughness are procedural).
- Animations: Universal Animation Library 1 and 2 (Standard) by Quaternius, CC0
  (https://quaternius.com), retargeted and restyled by 06_anim.py.

Hair cards, dress, belt, eyelets, lacing, necklace, handbag and all outfit textures
are generated procedurally by the pipeline and belong to the project.
