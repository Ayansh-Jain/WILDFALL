The commander (commander.glb) is generated by the scripts in Art/commander/pipeline
(Blender 5.2 + MPFB). Third-party sources:

- Body, eyes, eyebrows (eyebrow008), eyelashes, teeth, knee boots (culturalibre_male_boots),
  gloves (toigo_gloves_short) and the 52 ARKit face-unit shape keys: MakeHuman / MPFB
  community assets, CC0 (system assets released as CC0 in September 2020).
- Skin base texture: "skin male african middleage" by Mindfront, CC0
  (recoloured to a warm tan; stubble and the left-cheek scar are painted by 02_skin.py).
- Animations: Universal Animation Library 1 and 2 (Standard) by Quaternius, CC0
  (https://quaternius.com), retargeted by 06_anim.py.
- Griffin emblem on the cloak and brooch: "Griffin symbol" by Delapouite,
  https://game-icons.net, licensed CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
  Attribution is required in the game's credits.

Hair cards, outfit, armour, weapons, cloak drape and all outfit textures are generated
procedurally by the pipeline and belong to the project.
